# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Cards:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin

Visuals:
- Monthly Sales & Profit trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region

Slicers:
- Year
- Region
- Category
- Segment

## Page 2 — Product & Profitability
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit
- Discount vs Profit scatter
- Category / Sub-Category matrix

## Page 3 — Customers & Operations
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Top 10 Customers by Profit
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Average Shipping Days
- State-level Sales/Profit map

## Key Business Insights to Highlight
1. Revenue concentration across categories and regions.
2. Products/sub-categories with negative profitability.
3. Relationship between discounting and profit.
4. High-value customers.
5. Shipping performance differences.
