# 📊 Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

A business analytics project focused on understanding retail sales performance, profitability, customer value, product performance, regional trends, discount impact, and shipping efficiency.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze retail transaction data and identify the key factors influencing revenue and profitability.

The analysis answers practical business questions such as:

- Which categories and sub-categories generate the most profit?
- Which products or segments are underperforming?
- Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- How does discounting affect profitability?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which shipping methods have the best delivery performance?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** — Data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI calculation and visualization
- **Pandas** — Data manipulation and aggregation
- **SQL** — Business-focused analytical queries
- **Power BI** — Interactive business dashboard
- **Excel** — Data exploration and supporting analysis
- **Streamlit** — Interactive analytical dashboard
- **Matplotlib** — Data visualization
